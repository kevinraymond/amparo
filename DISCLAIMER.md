## Consent and authorization are a condition of use

Amparo is intended **only** for use with the informed, ongoing consent and
authorization of the account holder whose credentials are stored. The intended
model is: the account holder (the "member") owns their account; a helper they
choose (the "caregiver") assists at the member's direction. The enrollment flow
records an attestation of this consent on the device.

We recommend pairing any caregiving arrangement of this kind with appropriate
durable legal authority (for example, a durable financial power of attorney)
